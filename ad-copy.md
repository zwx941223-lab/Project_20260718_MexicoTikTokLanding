## Image prompts

### TikTok ad image, 4:5

Create a high-conversion vertical social ad for Mexico, aspect ratio 4:5, designed for TikTok and mobile feeds. Show a confident young Mexican small-business owner inside a bright neighborhood shop or mini bodega, smiling and holding a smartphone that displays a simple storefront interface. Use bold Mexico-inspired visual language: strong red, green, white, warm gold accents, hand-painted brush banners, retail icons, cardboard shipping boxes, subtle papel picado motifs, clean merchandising shelves, and a lively local commerce atmosphere. Large Spanish headline blocks with strong hierarchy: "ABRE TU TIENDA EN TIKTOK SHOP". Secondary support text: "Te apoyamos con registro y operacion". Add four clean feature cards: "Registro guiado", "Configuracion inicial", "Operacion diaria", "Apoyo en envios". Strong WhatsApp CTA band at the bottom: "ESCRIBENOS POR WHATSAPP". Make it feel polished, premium, energetic, and very local to Mexico, with crisp typography, realistic human details, and a feed-ready marketing layout.

### Landing hero image, square-ish portrait

Create a premium commercial lifestyle image for a Mexico-localized landing page. Show a Mexican entrepreneur in a warm, stylish small shop, standing beside shelves with products and packages, holding a phone with an online store interface visible. Visual tone should be trustworthy, modern, and optimistic, with subtle Mexican design references like handcrafted textures, market colors, or papel picado-inspired patterns in the background. Use natural light, realistic skin tones, polished product styling, and a clean e-commerce mood. The framing should leave room for overlay text, and the overall look should feel like a modern agency campaign for local merchants entering TikTok Shop.
